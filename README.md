Vice Cover Maker turns your own photos and a caption into GTA-style multi-panel cover art, entirely offline in your browser. Load one photo or several, drop them into five poster layouts (Vice Collage, Split Screen, Triptych, Hero + Inset, Comic Grid), drag and zoom each panel's photo into place, edit the arced gradient caption, sub-caption and starburst badge, pick a colour grade (Vice Sunset, Neon Nights, Golden Palms), and export a PNG at 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080. An original design in the spirit of the genre: no logos, artwork or fonts from any game, and your photos never leave your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser
```
